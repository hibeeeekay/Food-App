Our Story

Meet the founder

More than 10 years of home-style cooking, careful baking and celebrations served right. This is the woman whose standards run through every order we make.

10+Years in Business

The heart behind every tray and every cake

Sweet Flavour Magazzino began more than 10 years ago with one woman, a love for cooking and a simple promise: that every family deserves food that tastes homemade and looks like a celebration. What started in her own kitchen has grown into a full catering house, trusted for birthdays, weddings, naming ceremonies and big parties across Ipaja Ayobo and Lagos.

Our founder and CEO still leads the kitchen the way she always has. She chooses the ingredients, tastes every pot and trains her team to cook with the same care she serves at her own table. To her, a party is never just an order. It is someone's special day, and it has to be right.

That is why families come back year after year, and why customers recommend us to their friends. From the first phone call to the last tray delivered, you are dealing with a team that has done this for more than a decade and treats every event like it carries our name, because it does.

Good to know

For celebration cakes we recommend 3 to 5 days notice, and 1 to 2 weeks for multi-tier or heavily customised designs. For party catering, 1 to 2 weeks is ideal so we can plan properly, but for small chops, pastries and soup we can often deliver same day or next day. On a tight deadline? Still message us, we'll always tell you honestly what's possible.

Yes. For larger parties we can bring our team, pots and equipment to your venue and cook on-site, so the food is served fresh and hot. For smaller events we cook in our Ipaja Ayobo kitchen and deliver everything ready to serve. We'll advise which option suits your guest count and location.

Your soup is cooked to order, then packed in sealed food-safe containers while hot, wrapped for heat retention and sent straight to you. Locally in Ipaja, Ayobo and nearby areas, it arrives hot and ready to eat. Further across Lagos we send it insulated, and all soups reheat perfectly in a few minutes on the stove or in the microwave.

Absolutely, for weddings and large events we offer a paid tasting session for the dishes you're considering, and the tasting fee is credited against your final booking when you proceed with us. Just message us on WhatsApp to arrange a date.

We accept bank transfer, and confirmation is made on WhatsApp. For cakes and event catering, a 60% deposit secures your date, with the balance paid before delivery or collection. Smaller everyday orders (pastries, small chops, soup) are usually paid in full before delivery. We'll always send our account details to you directly from our official WhatsApp number, never from anywhere else.

Our home delivery covers Lagos. For neighbouring states we can arrange waybill dispatch through a trusted logistics partner. This works well for cakes and dry pastries like chin chin. Message us with your location and we'll confirm the best option and cost.
